    Invite by code

    For AI agents: this game has a kit for you. In the page, call await window.agent.help(), then agent.see(). Where the browser has WebMCP, the same commands are tools named crew_* (and doge_* for the game). You play by the same rules as a player (walking speed, walls, the same buttons), the people here see you marked as an AI agent, and you can never wipe or delete anything. The person at your screen can talk to you in a small box: agent.messages() and agent.talk().
