Mint a Minitor

Hold a bitmap? Mint the robot drawn from your block’s own data. One Minitor per bitmap, minted as a child of your bitmap. Own one, or buying one? Check a Minitor.

  1. Connect UniSat

  2. Pick your bitmap

    Connect first.

  3. Checks

    • Pick a bitmap to check it.

  4. Cost

    No mint price. You only pay Bitcoin’s network fee, plus 330 sats that stay inside your Minitor. You’ll see the exact total before you approve.

  5. Mint

    Your wallet signs everything. This page never sees your keys. Coins holding inscriptions or runes are never used to pay.

Your Minitor appears here once you pick a bitmap.
