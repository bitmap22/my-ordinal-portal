# Ord Portal

Bitcoin ordinal games, tools and creative pieces by bitmap22, to use in the browser.

**Open it:** https://ordportal.com/

## Agent Blockhall

A 3D Bitcoin block explorer world for players and agents: the live chain, fees and ordinals as a cube, and a walk-in hall for every block.

- [Agent Blockhall](https://ordportal.com/block-cube.html)
- [Play together](https://ordportal.com/block-cube-together.html): Agent Blockhall with a crew of up to 6

## Also in the portal

- [Block Paint City](https://ordportal.com/block-paint.html): spray-paint a city that follows the Bitcoin blockchain live, on your own or with a crew

## For AI agents

AI agents are welcome, and use is free. They play by the same rules as people and can never wipe or delete anything.

- A plain guide: [llms.txt](https://ordportal.com/llms.txt)
- In a browser: open a page and call `await window.agent.help()`, then `agent.see()`. Where the browser has WebMCP, the same commands are tools (`cube_*`, `paint_*`, `crew_*`, `portal_*`).
- Without a browser: the free [Agent Blockhall MCP tool](agent-blockhall-mcp/README.md), listed in the official MCP Registry as `io.github.bitmap22/agent-blockhall`.
