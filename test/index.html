    Every page here has a kit your agent can use. Without a browser, the free Agent Blockhall MCP tool gives it the same checks. Start with the guide for AI agents.

    await window.agent.help()

    Ordinal Viewer
    100% New tab
    On a computer, scroll inside the viewer to zoom. If a piece won't show here, open it in a new tab.
    For AI agents: this portal has a kit for you. In the page, call await window.agent.help(), then agent.see(). Where the browser has WebMCP, the same commands are tools named portal_*. Agent Blockhall, Block Paint, Doge Cube and their Play Together pages have full kits of their own once open; the portal's own tools get its page kit once open (tools named page_*). A plain guide is at llms.txt. You use the portal as a visitor does, and you can never wipe or delete anything. The person at your screen can talk to you in a small box: agent.messages() and agent.talk().
