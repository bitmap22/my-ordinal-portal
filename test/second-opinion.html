Second Opinion

Check a claim about Bitcoin against several sources

Type a claim about a block, an inscription, a collection, an address or a rune. The Second Opinion asks ord sites, mempool sites and the chain's own bytes, then says where they agree, where they don't, and why. It's free, for people and AI agents. To look closer, the answer links on to the Block Manifest, the Provenance Check, the Collection Check, Rune Proof and Agent Blockhall.

    What it can check

    Numbers can be written with commas. "About" or "around" before a number means within 1%. Inscriptions by number (#1000) or ID.

    For AI agents

    In this page: await window.agent.help(), then agent.check({claim}) (the claim in plain words, or {kind, subject, value}), agent.kinds() and agent.see(). Where the browser has WebMCP, the same commands are tools named opinion_*. They use the same button a person does, and the page says when an agent asked. Each answer carries cite: a link to cite the check, holding when it was made, which sites it asked, its main facts and a fingerprint; opening the link makes the check again and says whether it is the same, or what changed.

    The bigger app is Agent Blockhall. To prove more: the Block Manifest (a whole block), the Provenance Check (where an inscription came from), the Collection Check (a whole collection) and the Recursion Map (what an inscription loads).

    Without a browser: the free Agent Blockhall MCP tool has the same check as its secondOpinion tool. The reader is one file, opinion.js, shared by this page and the tool. More in llms.txt.

    Free, with no keys or accounts. It reads free public sites: ordinals.com (and its back-ups), ord.xverse.app, mempool.space (and its copies) and ordpool.space, gently: at most 30 asks at once, then 6 a second per site. Inscriptions belong to their creators: this page links to them and doesn't show them.
