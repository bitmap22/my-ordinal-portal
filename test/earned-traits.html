Earned Traits

What a bitmap and its holder have earned, proven on Bitcoin

Give a bitmap (like 388067.bitmap) or a wallet address. Earned Traits finds the inscription that really claimed each bitmap, then follows every move it has made since. Traits are earned only from what happened on the chain, and each one shows its proof. It only reads: you don't connect a wallet or sign anything.     What the traits mean

    ★ Blockout bitmap
    The district is one of blocks 0 to 796,515, and its claim was made in block 796,515 or earlier. Block 796,515 was mined on 30 June 2023, which the bitmap community calls Blockout Day: the day every block mined so far had been claimed.
    ★ Blockout claimer, never sold
    A blockout bitmap still held by the wallet that claimed it, which has never let it go: every move it ever made stayed in that same wallet. A bitmap that left, even if it came back later, doesn't earn it.
    ★ Held since claimed
    The same, for a bitmap of any year.
    ★ Held N years
    How long the wallet holding it now has had it, counted from the day it arrived there and stayed.

    The claim: the first exact "N.bitmap" inscribed in block N or later. Below 942,000 it is read from bop's on-chain index (OCI), and from 942,000 up from the blocks themselves; either way checked with ord. The trail is followed from where it sits now back to the claim, each step spending the one before it, at most 50 moves. More traits will come.

    For AI agents

    In this page: await window.agent.help(), then agent.bitmap({bitmap}), agent.wallet({address}) and agent.see(). Where the browser has WebMCP, the same commands are tools named traits_*. They use the same button a person does, and the page says when an agent asked. Nothing here signs or sends anything.

    The bigger app is Agent Blockhall: a bitmap's block is a hall you can walk into. The Provenance Check proves any one inscription step by step, and Track says the moment one moves.

    Without a browser: the free Agent Blockhall MCP tool has the same as its earnedTraits tool. The reader is one file, traits.js, shared by this page and the tool. More in llms.txt.
