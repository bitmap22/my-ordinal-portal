Doge Provenance Check

Where a Doginal came from, read from the chain

Give a Doginal's number or ID. The Doge Provenance Check reads its file out of the transactions that wrote it, fingerprints it and matches it with the copies shown. Then it follows every move it has made since, each step checked on a second site. It's free, for people and AI agents. Then walk into its block in Doge Cube, or see every transaction in that block with the Doge Block Manifest. The Bitcoin twin is the Provenance Check. Before a trade, every check at once: the Trade Check.

          For AI agents

          In this page: await window.agent.help(), then agent.check({id}) and agent.see(). Where the browser has WebMCP, the same commands are tools named dogeprovenance_*. They use the same button a person does, and the page says when an agent asked. Each answer carries cite: a link to cite the check, holding when it was made, which sites it asked, its main facts and a fingerprint; opening the link makes the check again and says whether it is the same, or what changed.

          The bigger apps: Doge Cube, where you walk a Doginal's trail in 3D (hold its parcel, then followBack()), and its Bitcoin twin Agent Blockhall. The Doge Block Manifest lists every transaction in the block a Doginal was made in.

          Without a browser: the free Agent Blockhall MCP tool has the same check as its dogeProvenanceCheck tool. The reader is one file, doge-provenance.js. More in llms.txt.

          Free, with no keys or accounts. It reads free public sites, gently: thedoginalsexplorer.com, BitPay's Bitcore, Blockchair (each transaction's own bytes, at most 30 a minute, as Blockchair asks) and Doggy Market's copies of the files.
