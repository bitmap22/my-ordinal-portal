Where it can, it proves what a site says from the chain's own bytes, or asks a second site. Where it can't, it says so. As of 24 November 2026.

    Strengths

    • Only sites whose terms allow the portal's use are read.
    • Blocks and transactions are proved from their own bytes (hash, transaction list, transaction IDs) before facts are shown.
    • Blocks, transactions and fees have 4 sites to try, asked politely, with busy sites rested.
    • The price comes from 2 separate sources, and the page says whether they agree.
    • The Ask more sources button (Block Manifest, Provenance Check, and the agent tool’s moreSources) checks with every extra site we can use, for anyone happy to wait a few more seconds.
    • Inscriptions are matched with the chain’s own bytes. A collection is proven only when the parent was spent to make the child.
    • Gaps are shown, never guessed: “not checked”, a blank fee with its reason, a site that didn’t answer.
    • The Truth League scores each site against proof from the chain, not against other sites, and Source Health checks every hour which sites are up. Pages and the free agent tool ask the sites that tell the truth and keep up first. A site is moved to the back, never dropped.

    Weaknesses

    • Some things have one source: ordinals.com (inscription records, its lists, inscription and sat numbers, rune supply) and OpenTimestamps tags.
    • Some numbers can’t be proved by anyone: inscription and sat numbers and rune supply come only from ordinals.com’s own counting.
    • 3 of the 4 Bitcoin sites run the same kind of software, so their agreeing is not fully separate. Each block’s bytes are still proved by its own hash, and blockstream.info runs other software.
    • Many sites can’t be used, because their terms don’t allow it, they don’t let web pages read them, or they now need a paid key. Stamp numbers, SRC-20 totals, Counterparty details and rare sat prices are not shown for this reason.
    • GitHub’s jobs and Agent Blockhall’s main block reader keep a fixed order of sites. They don’t use the Truth League’s order.
