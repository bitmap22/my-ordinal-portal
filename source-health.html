Source Health

Which Bitcoin data sites are answering

The portal's tools read free public data sites, with back-ups for when one is down. Source Health asks each one small thing and shows which answer, how fast, and whether they're up to date: now, from your browser, and over the last day and week, from GitHub's computers. So you, or an AI agent, know how far to trust an answer and which site to ask. Whether their answers are right: the Truth League. It's free. The tools it checks for: Agent Blockhall, the Block Manifest, the Provenance Check, the Second Opinion and the rest.

What it means

    Take it with you

    Every site: what it's used for, what it said now and how fast, and its last day and week from GitHub's computers.

    How it asks

    One small ask per site, all at the same time: most are asked for the newest block's height, which shows both that the site answers and whether it has kept up. The newest block is the highest height at least two sites have reached. GitHub's computers ask the same, about once an hour, and keep a week.

    For AI agents

    In this page: await window.agent.help(), then agent.check({chain}) (asks every site now, from this browser), agent.record({chain, site}) (GitHub's last day and week; give a site for its every hourly check) and agent.see(). Where the browser has WebMCP, the same commands are tools named health_*. They use the same button a person does, and the page says when an agent asked. Each answer carries cite: a link to cite the check, holding when it was made, which sites it asked, its main facts and a fingerprint; opening the link makes the check again and says whether it is the same, or what changed.

    The bigger app is Agent Blockhall, whose kit's health() says how each source answered it while you play. To check an answer: the Second Opinion (a claim against several sources), the Block Manifest (a whole block) and the Provenance Check (where an inscription came from).

    Without a browser: the free Agent Blockhall MCP tool has the same check as its sourceHealth tool, asked from your own computer. The reader is one file, health.js, shared by this page, the tool and GitHub's hourly check, whose record is on the source-health branch (week.json). More in llms.txt.

    Free, with no keys or accounts. One small ask per site when the page opens, and again only when you press Check again (at most once every 30 seconds).
