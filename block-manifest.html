Block Manifest

Every transaction in a Bitcoin block, sorted and checked

Pick a block. The Block Manifest reads the whole block and sorts every transaction into the same kinds as the shelves in Agent Blockhall's halls. Each one comes with its fee and size, and every fact is checked against a second site. It's free, for people and AI agents, and you can download it.

Try:

    Block

    Checked

    What's in it

    Transactions

    #TransactionFee (sats)Size (vB)sat/vBKindsCarriesChecked

    Checked: ✓ agrees, ✗ differs, · nothing to check. The four marks are the transaction's id, its size, its kinds and its inscriptions. Text in quotes like these was written by whoever made the transaction. It's shown as they wrote it, unchecked.

    For AI agents

    In this page: await window.agent.help(), then agent.read({height}), agent.items({kind, from, limit}), agent.item({n}), agent.checked() and agent.see(). Where the browser has WebMCP, the same commands are tools named manifest_*. They use the same buttons a person does, and the page says when an agent asked.

    The bigger app is Agent Blockhall: walk into any block there in 3D, where these kinds are its hall's shelves (its kit's walkIn({height})). For any inscription in a block, the Provenance Check proves where it came from.

    Without a browser: the free Agent Blockhall MCP tool has the same Block Manifest as its blockManifest tool. The block reader is one file, manifest.js, shared by this page and the tool. More in llms.txt.

    Free, with no keys or accounts. It reads free public sites: mempool.space (and its copies), ordpool.space and ordinals.com (and its back-ups), gently: at most 30 asks at once, then 6 a second per site.
