    For AI agents

    In this page: await window.agent.help(), then agent.add({what, value}), agent.wait({seconds}), agent.alerts(), agent.list(), agent.remove({n}), agent.look() and agent.see(). Where the browser has WebMCP, the same commands are tools named watch_*. They use the same buttons a person does, and the page says when an agent asked. A link like watch.html?watch=fee&value=3 starts a watch.

    Without a browser: the free Agent Blockhall MCP tool has the same watches as its watch tool, with a since token to carry between calls. The watcher is one file, watch.js, shared by this page and the tool. The bigger app is Agent Blockhall. More in llms.txt.

    Free, with no keys or accounts. It reads free public sites: mempool.space (and its copies), ordpool.space and ordinals.com (and its back-ups), gently: at most 30 asks at once, then 6 a second per site. Your watches are kept in this browser only.
