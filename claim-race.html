Claim Race

Bitmap claims waiting to be mined, watched live. For each one: is its number already taken, and is another claim for the same number waiting with a higher fee? Only the first claim of a number is a bitmap; a later one is a copy.

Read first: claims appear as soon as mempool.space reports them, so you see them fast. Each one says "checking…" until a second site (mempool.emzy.de) agrees, and only then gets its tick. Some claims may not be confirmed yet, and some may not be mined at all. This page only shows what we can see right now.

How it works, and what it can't see

  • The live feed is mempool.space's: new waiting transactions arrive in batches about every 10 seconds, each with its bytes. Each claim is read from its own bytes by the Block Manifest's reader (the halls' rules: the body exactly "N.bitmap", no delegate, no compression).
  • Each claim is also looked up on a second site (mempool.emzy.de, its own node) to show it is really waiting there too.
  • Taken or not: below block 942,000, bop's on-chain index (checked with ord); from 942,000, the portal's Bitmap Record, and for the newest blocks the blocks themselves.
  • Fee: what the claim pays per vbyte, counting any child transaction paying for it. Higher usually wins, but a miner decides: nothing here is certain until it is mined.
  • It can't see transactions sent straight to a miner, the first seconds before mempool.space hears of one, and when the page opens it reads only the next 8 blocks' worth of waiting transactions (mempool.space's projected blocks): a claim waiting further back is seen only if it moves up.
