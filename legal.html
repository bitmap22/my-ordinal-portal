5. Inscriptions and content made by others

The blockchain is public, and anyone can put anything on it. Inscriptions, names, messages, pictures and files shown on the portal belong to their own creators and owners. They are shown as they are found on the chain. We do not make them, check them or endorse them, and some may be offensive, misleading, unsafe or unlawful. For your safety, Agent Blockhall and Doge Cube never run an inscription's code: a web page or script inscription is not opened there, and links to its own page on ordinals.com (with ordiscan.com as a back-up) or the Doginals explorer instead. If you open one anywhere else, you do so at your own risk.

18+ content and the Family Guard. To keep the portal family friendly, every picture and film from an inscription is checked in your own browser before it is shown clearly (in Agent Blockhall, Doge Cube, their Play Together pages and the Sentinel ID Viewer). While it is checked it is blurred. If the checker thinks it may be adult content, it stays hidden behind an 18+ warning: only show it if you are 18 or over. Words in text inscriptions, player names and chat have rude words starred out. Family mode (the shield button, top right in Agent Blockhall and Doge Cube) hides every picture from an inscription until you choose. The checkers are free tools (NSFWJS and AllProfanity) and are not perfect: some adult or offensive content may still get through, and some harmless pictures may be hidden. Links to other sites, such as ordinals.com, open pages we do not check. Nothing is sent anywhere: every check happens on your own device.

The Safety Scan. About every hour, GitHub's computers look at and read every inscription of the latest Bitcoin and Dogecoin blocks, without running any of it, and mark what to watch out for with a colour and a symbol: may be adult, may flash (a seizure risk), may be a scam, agent traps (words or code that try to give AI agents orders), risky code, miners or phone-killers, hate words and personal details. The marks show on the Safety Scan pages and over the parcels in Agent Blockhall and Doge Cube. Every mark is a "may be": the scan uses free tools and simple rules, so it can mark harmless things and miss harmful ones, and no mark does not mean a file is safe. It is a warning to help you, not a judgement about anyone, and not advice. The pages never show or run an inscription; phone numbers, emails and hate words are masked so the scan never spreads them further. If you think something is marked wrongly, or a mark is missing, tell us.

If you believe something shown here infringes your rights, please tell us through Report a problem.
