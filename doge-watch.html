Doge Watch

Know the moment something happens on Dogecoin, every alert checked

Pick what to watch. While this page is open, it looks every 30 seconds and tells you the moment it happens, with a sound or a pop-up if you like. Every alert is checked: a new block against its own header and a second site, a move or a payment on two sites. From each one, walk into the block in Doge Cube, see every transaction with the Doge Block Manifest, or prove a Doginal's past with the Doge Provenance Check. The Bitcoin twin is the Watch.

    For AI agents

    In this page: await window.agent.help(), then agent.add({what, value}), agent.wait({seconds}), agent.alerts(), agent.list(), agent.remove({n}), agent.look() and agent.see(). Where the browser has WebMCP, the same commands are tools named dogewatch_*. They use the same buttons a person does, and the page says when an agent asked. A link like doge-watch.html?watch=inscription&value=%230 starts a watch.

    Without a browser: the free Agent Blockhall MCP tool has the same watches as its dogeWatch tool. The watcher is one file, doge-watch.js. The bigger app is Doge Cube, and its Bitcoin twin is Agent Blockhall. More in llms.txt.

    Free, with no keys or accounts. It reads free public sites, gently: thedoginalsexplorer.com, BitPay's Bitcore and Blockchair (a kind watch reads the carrying transactions of each new block from their own bytes, at most 30 a minute, as Blockchair asks).
