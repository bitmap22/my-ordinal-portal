Provenance Check

Where a Bitcoin inscription came from, every step checked

Give an inscription's number or ID. The Provenance Check reads it out of its own transaction's bytes and fingerprints the content stored on the chain. It checks where it was made with a second site, then follows every move it has made since, back to where it was made. It's free, for people and AI agents.

        For AI agents

        In this page: await window.agent.help(), then agent.check({id}) and agent.see(). Where the browser has WebMCP, the same commands are tools named provenance_*. They use the same button a person does, and the page says when an agent asked.

        Without a browser: the free Agent Blockhall MCP tool has the same check as its provenanceCheck tool, and it can also unpack inscriptions stored packed. The reader is one file, provenance.js, shared by this page and the tool. More in llms.txt.

        Free, with no keys or accounts. It reads free public sites: ordinals.com (and its back-ups) and mempool.space (and its copies), gently: at most 30 asks at once, then 6 a second per site. Inscriptions belong to their creators: this page links to them and doesn't show them.
